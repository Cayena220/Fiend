# Changelog

Each version's bullets are copied into `web/downloads/release.json` by `scripts/build.ps1`
and shown in Fiend's update popup, so write them for the person reading that popup.

## 1.2.0

- Fiend now checks for updates when it opens and offers to install them in one click.
- Updates are downloaded, checked against their published SHA-256, and installed silently, and Fiend reopens on its own.
- Check for updates any time, or switch the check off, under Customize › About.

## 1.1.0

- Drive health: life left, temperature, hours powered on, data written and bad sectors for every drive.
- A wear trend for each drive, recorded once a day.
- Drive life now counts toward the Overview health score.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Cleaner, Disk, Shield, Quarantine and Customize.
